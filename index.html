Questions, answered

Is this really async?

Yes — nobody joins a call. Updates are written when it suits each person and read when it suits everyone else.

What if someone forgets to post?

They get one gentle nudge at digest time. Missed days show as a quiet dot, not a scarlet letter.

Can we export our history?

Any time. One click gets you every update as JSON or Markdown.
